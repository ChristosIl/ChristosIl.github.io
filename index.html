About Me

I was born on February 4, 2001, in Alexandroupolis, and for the past ten years I have been living in Athens, initially moving here after receiving a sports scholarship to attend the Moraitis School. From a young age, I have been fascinated by technology and developed a strong passion for computers and the sciences. In 2020, I joined the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE) at the National Technical University of Athens through the national university entrance exams. Outside of academics, volleyball has been a big part of my life since childhood, and in recent years I have competed at a semi-professional level. I also enjoy sports in general, traveling, and reading crime novels, which allow me to balance my technical interests with creative and personal hobbies.
